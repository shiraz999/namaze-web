Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NAMAZI ("we", "our", "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application and related services. By using NAMAZI, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: name, phone number, date of birth, occupation, preferred language, and your home location, provided when you create an account or sign in (including via Google Sign-In).
  • Location data: with your permission, we access your device's precise location to find nearby masjids, calculate local (Awwal) prayer times, show the Qibla direction, and fill in your country, state and district. Your location is sent to our servers when you sign up and when you search for nearby masjids.
  • Background location (auto-silent): if you turn on auto-silent, the app uses your location in the background, even when the app is closed, to detect when you arrive at or leave a masjid so it can silence and restore your phone. This arrival detection runs on your device. You can turn auto-silent off or withdraw the permission at any time in your device settings.
  • Do Not Disturb access (Android): used only to switch your phone to vibrate or silent inside a masjid and to restore your ringer afterwards.
  • Photos: images you choose to upload, such as photos of a masjid when creating or updating a masjid listing, or attachments to broadcast posts.
  • Device and notification data: a push notification token, your device type (Android or iOS) and device model, used to deliver prayer-time updates and masjid broadcast alerts.
  • Masjid information: if you register or manage a masjid, we collect the masjid name, address, GPS location, prayer times and contact details you submit, linked to your account. Your name and phone number are visible to the moderators who review new masjids so they can contact you if needed.
  • Data saved on your device: your followed masjids, their prayer times and images are cached on your phone so the app keeps working offline. Prayer alarms are scheduled on your device.

How We Use Your Information

  • To show you nearby masjids and accurate local prayer times.
  • To send azan reminders and masjid broadcast notifications.
  • To silence your phone inside a masjid when you turn on auto-silent.
  • To let you create, manage, and personalize your account and masjid listings.
  • To operate and improve the core functionality of the app.

Third Parties

We use Google Sign-In for authentication and Firebase Cloud Messaging (a Google service) to deliver push notifications. Maps and directions are provided by Google Maps (or Apple Maps on iPhone). The Islamic calendar uses aladhan.com for Hijri dates (only dates are sent) and date.nager.at for public holidays (only your country code is sent). Your data is otherwise stored on our own backend servers. We do not use advertising networks, and we do not use any third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Data Storage & Security

Your information is stored on our own servers and protected with reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, email us at support@namazi-app.com. We will process your request within a reasonable timeframe.

Children's Privacy

NAMAZI is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy
